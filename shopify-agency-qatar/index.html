Shopify design and development for businesses in Qatar.

FIKRA is a Shopify design and development agency serving businesses in Qatar. We help brands launch a new online store, redesign an existing storefront or plan a move to Shopify. Projects can include Arabic and English layouts, product and collection pages, and payment and delivery integrations agreed for the store.

What we can help you build

Our work covers new Shopify stores, redesigns, migration planning, bilingual storefronts, payment and delivery integrations, and improvements to existing product pages and mobile layouts. The project starts with your catalogue, brand assets and the way customers shop. A written scope identifies the pages, features and responsibilities included.

Arabic and English shopping experiences

An Arabic storefront needs more than translated text. We consider right-to-left layouts, menus, product information and the readability of both languages on mobile. We agree who provides and approves the translations before adding them to the store.

Payments and delivery for your market

We review payment providers and delivery services available to your business. Apple Pay and Google Pay can be included through a provider that supports the merchant, country and store setup. Eligibility, onboarding and any provider fees need confirmation; a logo alone does not enable a payment method.

Project budget and scope

FIKRA Shopify store projects start from QAR 5,000. Your quote depends on the catalogue, pages, custom features, migration requirements and agreed scope. Shopify subscriptions, paid themes, apps and payment-provider fees may be separate. We confirm those responsibilities before work begins.

How the first conversation works

Submit your name, phone number and project details using the call request form. We call you to discuss your products, audience, existing website and priorities. If a meeting would be useful, we can arrange it after that conversation. The call request does not reserve a calendar slot.

What to prepare for a useful brief

Share your logo, product photos and descriptions, product options, collection structure, delivery information and any existing store URL. For a redesign or migration, identify the pages and functions you need to preserve. Timing is agreed after we review the scope and available content.

Questions about this service

Does FIKRA work with businesses in Qatar?

Yes. FIKRA provides Shopify design and development services for businesses in Qatar, including new stores, redesigns and bilingual storefronts. The agreed scope depends on your products, content and requirements.

How much does a Shopify store project cost?

FIKRA Shopify store projects start from QAR 5,000. We confirm the final quote after reviewing the catalogue, pages and features. Subscription, theme, app and provider costs may be separate.

Can you add Apple Pay and Google Pay?

We can include setup through a supported payment provider. Availability depends on the provider, your merchant eligibility, country and store configuration. These requirements are reviewed before the integration is included in the scope.

Can you redesign a store without replacing every part of it?

Yes. We can agree a focused scope for selected pages, navigation or custom sections. We first review the existing theme, apps, product options and content to identify what needs to be preserved.

Do I need to book a time to ask about my project?

No. Submit a call request with your phone number and project details. We will call to discuss the project and arrange a meeting afterward if needed.
