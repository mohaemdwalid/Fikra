Give your existing Shopify store a clearer shopping experience.

A Shopify redesign starts with what your existing store needs. FIKRA reviews the storefront, navigation and product pages, then helps you define changes that fit your brand and the way your customers shop.

When a redesign is useful

Your catalogue may have grown, your brand may have changed, or mobile pages may feel difficult to browse. A redesign can address how collections are organised, how product information is presented and how visitors move through the store. We discuss the problems before deciding which pages to change.

Define the work before changing the theme

A visual refresh does not always require a full rebuild. The project may involve selected templates, navigation or custom sections. Existing theme features, apps, product options and content need to be reviewed so the agreed design fits the store's requirements.

Content and functionality belong together

Product photography, descriptions, size information and delivery details influence how clearly shoppers understand your offer. We consider these alongside layout and mobile behaviour. Any content gaps, app changes or additional features are included in planning rather than assumed to be part of every project.

Measure changes with context

A redesign cannot promise a particular conversion rate or sales result. If you have analytics, they can help identify pages worth reviewing and provide a baseline. Traffic quality, pricing, promotions, product demand and seasonality also affect the outcome. Use comparable periods and clear definitions when reviewing performance.

Questions about this service

Can you redesign only part of the store?

Yes, a focused scope can cover selected pages or templates. We agree what needs to change after reviewing the store.

Will a redesign guarantee more sales?

No. Design can improve clarity and usability, but commercial results also depend on your products, offer and traffic. Any measured claim needs supporting data.

What should I share before the consultation?

Share your store URL, the pages causing concern, your priorities and any relevant analytics you are comfortable discussing. Do not send account passwords.
