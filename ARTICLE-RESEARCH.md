# Article research and editorial scope

Research date: October 16, 2026.

## Selection method

The existing 40 Shopify questions were extended with 60 distinct questions: 15 business-website topics, 15 platform-selection topics, 15 Qatar business topics and 15 operational/advanced topics. Search results informed local relevance, while current primary documentation was used to verify platform and authority details. The advice and examples are original editorial planning guidance.

No Google Keyword Planner, Search Console query export, paid keyword database or Qatar search-volume dataset was available. Consequently, no search volumes, popularity ranks or difficulty scores were invented, and the collection is not described as the 100 most searched questions. Use the owner’s future Search Console data to improve priorities and expand useful answers.

## Source standards

Platform comparisons use documentation from the named platforms, not competitor affiliate rankings. Shopify versus WordPress distinguishes WordPress, WooCommerce and the selected hosting arrangement. Wix and Squarespace comparisons focus on requirements and actual configuration. Current Shopify B2B documentation was checked rather than repeating outdated Plus-only assumptions.

Qatar registration, domain and trustmark questions cite MOCI, MCIT/ecommerce.gov.qa and CRA. These articles explain how to verify a relevant route; they do not invent activity-specific licenses, legal fees, tax rates, approval guarantees or FIKRA credentials. Changeable information must be rechecked before a real commercial or legal decision.

Articles contain no fabricated customer testimonials, commercial results, search endorsements or claims of universal platform superiority. General website guides do not establish that FIKRA supplies WordPress implementation services. The actual booking CTA remains a Shopify consultation.

OpenAI crawler guidance is used only for ChatGPT search access; access is not represented as a guarantee of inclusion, recommendation or ranking.

## Editorial revision — October 16, 2026

Replaced all 100 Arabic question titles and bilingual openings. Added a topic-specific explanation/example and a separate practical followup to each topic in both languages, 17 decision/comparison tables and further extended prose in six complex guides. Revised article rendering removes repeated short-answer labels, checklist blocks and mini FAQs. Original explanatory sections remain, rather than claiming every paragraph was rewritten. Examples describe hypothetical situations; they are not FIKRA client experiences or measured results.

Arabic questions use a conversational regional register, while the answers use clear Arabic rather than literal English sentence order. No claim of human authorship is made. Current official Shopify Payments country guidance, WordPress/WooCommerce documentation and Qatar MOCI/CRA sources were consulted for the expanded comparisons and local context.
