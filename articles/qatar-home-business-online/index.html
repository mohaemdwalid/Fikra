Doing business in Qatar

What should a home-based Qatar business verify before selling online?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Confirm the requirements for your own activity and circumstances. Another home seller’s page is not evidence that the same route applies to you.

In this guide
  1. Describe the setup, not just “online selling”
  2. Ask a specific official question
  3. Plan privacy and customer contact
  4. Coordinate launch dependencies
  5. Separate business readiness from website readiness

Describe the setup, not just “online selling”

Explain what you make or resell, where it is prepared or stored and how customers receive it. Use that description when checking current official services or asking a qualified adviser. Separately plan public contact details, payment-provider requirements and delivery capacity. Do not expose unnecessary personal information to make the site appear complete. Keep approval tasks visible in the launch plan and publish only credentials actually issued to the business.

Ask a specific official question

Describe the product, preparation location, customer contact and delivery method. Use current MOCI channels to confirm the route relevant to your circumstances. Do not treat another seller’s social-media activity as evidence of permission for your own business.

Plan privacy and customer contact

Decide which contact channels and address information should be public and which details are only needed operationally. Use genuine business details without exposing unnecessary personal information. Review obligations with an appropriate adviser where needed.

Coordinate launch dependencies

Confirm payment-provider requirements, fulfilment capacity and approved customer information. Keep registration or approval tasks separate from design tasks. Only publish claims of licensing or certification that are actually supported by issued records.

Separate business readiness from website readiness

A working checkout does not answer whether your activity, location or payment account is eligible. List those questions for the relevant authority and provider before commissioning the store. In parallel, prepare a realistic catalogue and fulfilment plan so technical progress does not hide an unresolved business dependency.
