Payments, delivery and launch

How should you set up cash on delivery on Shopify?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Set up the payment choice and the collection process together. Receiving an order does not mean the courier has collected its value.

In this guide
  1. Follow the money as well as the parcel
  2. Do not collapse these order states
  3. Explain the conditions clearly
  4. Separate order creation from payment
  5. Monitor the operational cost
  6. Reconcile the money after delivery

Follow the money as well as the parcel

Agree where COD is available and what instructions the courier needs. Then define who records delivery, who matches settlements and who updates payment status. A failed delivery and a delivered-but-unreconciled payment are different issues. Keep those stages separate in your review. Test the customer instructions and any real additional charge before publishing. Compare collected results with fulfilment costs rather than counting every placed COD order as paid revenue.

Explain the conditions clearly

State where COD is available, what the customer needs to prepare and whether your actual delivery arrangement has an additional charge. Confirm the instructions with your courier. Keep the wording near the payment choice so customers do not discover important conditions only after placing an order.

Separate order creation from payment

Shopify manual-payment orders can remain unpaid until the money is received and recorded. An order confirmation is therefore not evidence that a COD payment was collected. Agree who updates payment status and how the team matches courier settlements to orders.

Monitor the operational cost

Track dispatches, successful deliveries, failed attempts and collected amounts using consistent definitions. Compare COD results with the costs of fulfilment and returns. If a destination causes repeated issues, review the process and coverage rather than assuming all placed orders represent completed revenue.

Reconcile the money after delivery

Define how an order moves from placed to delivered, refused or returned and who confirms the collected amount. Include collection fees and remittance timing in your operational review. A storefront payment label does not by itself create a reliable cash record between your team and the delivery partner.
