Doing business in Qatar

Does a Qatar B2B supplier need checkout or a quotation website?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Use checkout when the buyer can know the price and terms before ordering. Use a quote route when the commercial agreement needs discussion first.

In this guide
  1. What the buyer needs to send
  2. Map the commercial conversation
  3. Design a useful request
  4. Evaluate tools after the workflow
  5. Design the handoff after the form

What the buyer needs to send

If price depends on quantity, specifications or delivery timing, ask for those details instead of publishing a misleading fixed number. Keep the first request manageable and explain who follows up. If account-based ordering is needed, review current B2B capabilities and plan requirements rather than assuming a custom portal is compulsory. Test one buyer’s journey from the catalogue to a useful quote or order, including the information your sales team actually receives.

Map the commercial conversation

Identify quantities, technical specifications, delivery scheduling and approval steps. Decide which information can be public and which needs a qualified enquiry. Avoid showing a fixed price when every transaction actually depends on negotiated scope.

Design a useful request

Collect the details needed to prepare a relevant quote without turning the first contact into a burdensome application. Explain response expectations and secure handling of documents. Route enquiries to an actual commercial owner.

Evaluate tools after the workflow

Review Shopify B2B capabilities and current plan dependencies if commerce is part of the brief. Do not assume capabilities from an old Plus-only comparison or promise features without checking the current setup. Test the buyer’s real task before investing in a portal.

Design the handoff after the form

Decide which details are needed to produce a quotation and which can wait for a conversation. Route each request to an owner and tell the customer what happens next. A quote button helps little if attachments disappear or sales staff cannot trace the original requirement.
