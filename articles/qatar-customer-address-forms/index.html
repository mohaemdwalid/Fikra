Doing business in Qatar

How should website forms handle Qatar addresses and phone numbers?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Ask the delivery team what it needs, then design the form around that. A template for another country may reject perfectly useful local information.

In this guide
  1. Test the awkward inputs
  2. Define operational fields
  3. Test realistic inputs
  4. Preserve and protect submissions
  5. Check what the courier actually needs

Test the awkward inputs

Try an Arabic name, an international phone number and an address mixing Arabic and English. Does the keyboard suit the field, and does the error explain how to fix it? Avoid forcing a foreign postcode pattern just because the original template used one. Distinguish delivery information from a quote enquiry, which may need much less. Keep entered data when submission fails and decide who can access it afterward.

Define operational fields

Confirm the address details your courier uses and which fields are necessary for a quote versus a delivery. Avoid asking for identity documents or unrelated personal data in an ordinary enquiry. Keep instructions clear in the languages you support.

Test realistic inputs

Review international phone format, Arabic names and mixed-language address text. Check keyboard behaviour and error messages on mobile. Do not enforce a foreign postcode pattern merely because it was present in a template.

Preserve and protect submissions

Keep entered information when validation fails and use appropriate access controls for stored data. W3C guidance supports clear labels and usable forms. Agree how the team corrects incomplete details without exposing customer information publicly.

Check what the courier actually needs

Ask the delivery team to review a sample order from the form. Identify missing details and unnecessary fields. Explain why any additional information is needed, especially location notes, and keep it available to the people fulfilling the order rather than collecting it without a clear purpose.
