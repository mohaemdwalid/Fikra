Shopping experience and growth

How do you plan Shopify discounts without confusing customers?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Give the promotion a purpose and calculate it with your own costs. More orders do not necessarily mean a better result.

In this guide
  1. Test the offer the customer will actually use
  2. Define what the offer is for
  3. Check the economics
  4. Test combinations and communication
  5. Test the awkward baskets

Test the offer the customer will actually use

Write eligible products, dates and limits before designing the banner. Try an eligible basket, an ineligible one and the exact discount combinations customers may enter. Include fulfilment and payment costs in the commercial review. Keep the advert, cart and checkout consistent, and remove expired claims afterward. If the real barrier is unclear product information, a broad discount may spend margin without solving the customer’s uncertainty.

Define what the offer is for

Decide whether the promotion supports a launch, a specific collection or another measurable goal. Record eligible products, customer conditions, dates and redemption limits where applicable. Do not use a crossed-out price or urgency message that misrepresents the actual offer.

Check the economics

Calculate the reduction alongside fulfilment, payment and product costs using your own numbers. A promotion can increase order count while reducing contribution. Review whether a free-shipping threshold or a product-specific offer better fits the goal instead of automatically discounting the entire catalogue.

Test combinations and communication

Review the current discount-combination rules for your setup. Try eligible and ineligible items, boundary basket values and any relevant automatic discounts. Make the advert, landing page, cart and checkout describe the same conditions. Keep someone responsible for removing expired banners after the campaign.

Test the awkward baskets

Try the discount with an excluded product, another promotion and a basket near any qualifying threshold. Confirm the total makes sense for both the customer and the business. Publish the conditions in clear language and decide how staff handle an order placed just before an offer expires.
