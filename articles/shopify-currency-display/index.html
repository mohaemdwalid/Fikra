Arabic and international selling

How should you plan currency display in a Shopify store?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Not necessarily. Display currency, checkout currency and the currency received by your business can be different. Check the payment route, not just the selector.

In this guide
  1. What the customer actually pays
  2. Write a currency map
  3. Check the full total
  4. Plan maintenance
  5. Follow the amount through checkout

What the customer actually pays

Choose a destination and compare the product price, cart total, shipping, discount and final payment currency. If the currency changes, the customer should understand when and why. Verify what your provider supports for your business and account; a display widget cannot create payment support. Keep a record of rounding and market-pricing settings, then recheck after changing providers, apps or the markets you serve.

Write a currency map

Record store currency, display currencies, checkout behaviour and payout currency for your intended markets. Review current provider support directly. A converted number shown by a theme widget is not proof that the payment processor will charge the customer in that currency.

Check the full total

Look beyond the product price. Review discounts, shipping, any applicable charges and the final checkout amount. Explain currency changes clearly if the journey requires them. Compare the customer-facing amount with an appropriate test result and avoid assuming that formatting alone creates local payment support.

Plan maintenance

Exchange-rate behaviour, price rounding and market-specific pricing need an owner. Keep a record of the settings used and review them when the payment provider or selling markets change. Test after app or theme changes that affect price presentation, particularly on bilingual pages with different number formatting.

Follow the amount through checkout

Choose a sample product and record the displayed amount, checkout currency and what your eligible payment route charges. Explain any difference before the customer commits. Currency presentation, settlement and bank conversion are separate questions; confirm the actual setup rather than inferring it from a selector.
