Business websites

What affects business website costs in Qatar?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

A useful price comparison starts with the pages, content and functions included. A cheap starting figure can leave important work outside the quote.

In this guide
  1. Make the quote readable
  2. Read the proposal line by line
  3. Define the deliverables
  4. Identify recurring responsibilities
  5. Compare complete proposals
  6. Ask suppliers to price the same job

Make the quote readable

Ask for distinct line items for page layouts, languages, copy preparation, forms, integrations and handover. Clarify whether a second language repeats the structure or needs different content. Then separate domain, hosting and maintenance renewals. A five-page site with approved text is not the same project as five pages requiring interviews, writing and photography. Compare the same brief, including exclusions, rather than choosing the smallest number without knowing what you will receive.

Define the deliverables

Count distinct page layouts, languages, forms and connections. A page assembled from supplied copy is different from a project requiring content preparation and photography. Ask what revisions, testing and handover are included.

Identify recurring responsibilities

Hosting arrangements differ by platform; WordPress documentation explains hosting as part of its setup. Record renewals, premium extensions and ongoing support separately. Confirm whether you can change supplier without losing account access.

Compare complete proposals

Give suppliers the same brief and list exclusions. Avoid treating a cheap starting price as the final project budget. FIKRA’s public guides do not establish a fixed market price; request actual quotations for your requirements.

Ask suppliers to price the same job

Give each supplier the same page list, language requirements and content responsibility. Ask what happens when a page needs a different layout or the copy arrives late. You will learn more from those answers than from comparing starting prices attached to different scopes.
