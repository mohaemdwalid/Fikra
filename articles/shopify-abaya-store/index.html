Products and collections

How should an abaya brand organise its Shopify store?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Give each design a clear reference and keep its photos, measurements and options together. Small differences matter when many garments have the same colour.

In this guide
  1. Start with the product record
  2. Keep each design identifiable
  3. Plan real buying options
  4. Support a confident choice
  5. Make product identity clear

Start with the product record

Create one record per design with front, back and detail images. Identify the cut or finishing that distinguishes it from similar pieces. Then record whether it is ready stock or made to order and which lengths can be purchased. Do not reuse a photo because another garment looks close. Test an order from the storefront through to picking: the team should know exactly which design and option the customer chose.

Keep each design identifiable

Give each design a stable name or reference and match its front, back and detail photos. Do not mix similar black garments just because their colours match. Record the distinguishing cut, embroidery or trim so product entry and later content updates preserve the correct identity.

Plan real buying options

Shopify variants can represent combinations such as size and colour. Decide whether length, fit or another choice belongs in the product structure, and confirm how it affects stock and production. Separate ready-stock and made-to-order expectations in your content without promising a dispatch time you cannot maintain.

Support a confident choice

Provide approved measurements, fabric information, care instructions and relevant delivery details. Review Arabic and English naming so the same design stays recognisable in both languages. Use collections that match shopper needs, such as everyday or occasion wear, only where the products actually fit those categories.

Make product identity clear

Keep views of the same design together and use options only for genuine variations of that product. Show the details customers ask about, such as fabric, length and included pieces, when accurate. A customer should not need to message you to discover which photograph matches the selected item.
