Shopping experience and growth

When should a Shopify campaign use a dedicated landing page?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Send people where the advert’s promise continues clearly. A dedicated landing page is useful when it explains something the product page does not.

In this guide
  1. Choose by what the visitor still needs
  2. Match the destination to the question
  3. Keep the offer consistent
  4. Measure the whole route
  5. Match the page to the promise

Choose by what the visitor still needs

An advert for one clearly defined item may work with a complete product page. A collection story or an offer needing several explanations may need a focused landing page. Keep the same identity, conditions and availability across the route. Test it on a phone with the exact campaign link. Compare completed outcomes as well as clicks before deciding that the extra page helped; adding another step can also introduce friction.

Match the destination to the question

A campaign for one clear product may work well with a complete product page. A collection story or offer needing explanation may benefit from a dedicated layout. Write down what the visitor already knows from the advert and what they still need before choosing the destination.

Keep the offer consistent

Use the same product identity, conditions and availability across the advert and page. Show the relevant proof and product details without inventing testimonials or urgency. If a promotion has an end date or eligibility condition, explain it where customers decide rather than only in a remote policy page.

Measure the whole route

Review landing engagement, product selection, checkout and completed orders as appropriate. Keep campaign tracking consistent and inspect mobile behaviour. Shopify theme customisation can support different layouts, but the result still depends on the offer and audience. Compare meaningful outcomes before keeping a dedicated page permanently.

Match the page to the promise

Read the ad beside the destination page. The same product, offer and next step should be easy to find without extra searching. Use a collection when people need a choice and a product page when the ad already chose the item. Measure the complete journey, not only the click rate.
