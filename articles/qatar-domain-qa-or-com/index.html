Doing business in Qatar

Should a Qatar business choose a .qa or .com domain?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Choose a name that fits the brand and intended audience, then check the conditions of that exact extension. The suffix alone does not create a business or a ranking.

In this guide
  1. Decide the primary address before launch
  2. Decide the audience
  3. Check registration conditions
  4. Keep the business in control
  5. Think about the address people will remember

Decide the primary address before launch

If Qatar is central to the brand, a local domain may be relevant; if you serve several markets, compare the wider naming plan too. CRA’s guidance distinguishes extensions and registration arrangements, so do not transfer one extension’s requirements to another. Check availability, the registered contact and renewal ownership. If you buy alternatives, choose one primary address and plan redirects instead of publishing duplicate sites with competing identities.

Decide the audience

A Qatar-focused identity and a wider international brand may prioritise different names. Check availability and avoid confusing similarities with other businesses. Do not promise first position in search merely because a domain uses .qa.

Check registration conditions

Use CRA’s current registrant guidance and accredited channels for the chosen extension. Confirm the applicant details and renewal process. Do not assume that requirements for .com.qa also describe every other extension.

Keep the business in control

Register with accurate business-controlled contact details and record renewal ownership. If you use more than one domain, decide the primary one and redirect alternatives appropriately. Review email and existing links before changing a live domain.

Think about the address people will remember

Compare a few readable names, not only extensions. Check spelling in conversation and how the address looks beside your email. Before purchasing, confirm current registration requirements and ownership arrangements. If you use more than one domain, decide which is the main public address and how the others lead to it.
