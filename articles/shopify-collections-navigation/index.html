Products and collections

How do you organise Shopify collections and navigation?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Use names customers recognise. The menu should help someone find a product without knowing your internal catalogue or campaign names.

In this guide
  1. Watch someone find a product
  2. Start with customer vocabulary
  3. Decide how membership is maintained
  4. Review the whole navigation route
  5. Sort by how customers ask

Watch someone find a product

Ask a person unfamiliar with the store to find an everyday abaya in their size. Notice which menu label they choose and where they hesitate. If the route needs an explanation from you, improve the labels or grouping. Also check how products enter each collection: inconsistent tags can leave relevant items out. Remove empty seasonal links when the season ends, and make it easy to return from a product to the collection.

Start with customer vocabulary

List the labels customers use in enquiries: everyday abayas, occasion wear or new arrivals, for example. Choose clear names and avoid making the entire menu depend on internal campaign names. A shopper should be able to predict what a link contains before opening it.

Decide how membership is maintained

Shopify supports manual and smart collections. Choose the approach that fits how reliably your team maintains product data. If a collection depends on a rule, document that rule and test products near its boundary. Avoid relying on inconsistent tags that make items disappear unexpectedly.

Review the whole navigation route

Move from the main menu to a collection, then to a product and back. Check how people find other relevant products without restarting from the homepage. Review empty collections, seasonal links and mobile menu depth. Keep navigation changes small enough to observe whether they reduce confusion.

Sort by how customers ask

Look at the terms customers use in messages and search. Build a small set of meaningful groups and test where a mixed product belongs. Separate navigation from promotional badges: a temporary campaign should not make the permanent catalogue impossible to understand after it ends.
