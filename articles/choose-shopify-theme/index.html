Planning your store

How do you choose a Shopify theme for your brand?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Try your own content in the theme. A demo built around another brand’s photography can make almost any layout look convincing.

In this guide

  2. What a theme demo should prove

A better test than browsing demos

Choose one typical product and one difficult product: a long name, several sizes or unusual image proportions. Preview both on a phone. Can you see the selected size, read the delivery information and browse the photos comfortably? Ask which changes are settings and which need development. This reveals the real fit before a purchase, rather than discovering afterward that the gallery or menu cannot support your catalogue.

Test a representative catalogue

Use a long product title, multiple photos, size options and a sold-out item. Check whether a collection remains easy to scan with your image proportions. For a bilingual store, review Arabic copy and direction as well. Keep a short scorecard so each candidate is judged against the same requirements.

Distinguish settings from custom work

Some changes are available in the theme editor; others require code or an app. Ask which of your requirements the theme handles directly and which need additional work. Shopify allows previews of themes, and paid Theme Store themes require purchase before publication. Verify licensing for the store that will use it.

Review the editing experience

The owner should understand how to change banners, arrange sections and update product content after handover. Ask for a preview using realistic material rather than approving a blank demo. Check support responsibilities and preserve the current live theme before making a replacement decision.

Use your hardest product in the demo

A simple product makes most themes look good. Try your longest title, most complicated options and largest navigation category. Review those on mobile in Arabic too. Keep a list of changes the demonstration needed so the theme price is not mistaken for the complete implementation cost.
