Doing business in Qatar

How should a Qatar business prepare its website for Ramadan or Eid campaigns?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Begin with stock and fulfilment capacity, then build the promotion around what you can deliver. A seasonal banner is the easy part.

In this guide
  1. Work backward from delivery
  2. Build a readiness schedule
  3. Keep the offer consistent
  4. Plan the end of the campaign
  5. Plan what happens when the campaign ends

Work backward from delivery

Confirm the courier’s actual arrangements and your preparation time before announcing a final-order deadline. Separate ready products from made-to-order items. Check the same offer in the advert, landing page and checkout, including the Arabic wording. Assign someone to remove expired messages. When reviewing results, record the stock and promotion changes; a seasonal increase does not by itself prove that a redesign caused the improvement.

Build a readiness schedule

Record product availability, creative approval, promotion rules and dispatch capacity. Assign an owner to each deadline. Confirm relevant holiday and courier arrangements with the actual providers before publishing a final-order date.

Keep the offer consistent

Match adverts, landing pages, product information and checkout. Explain included items, eligibility and any made-to-order timing. Review Arabic wording so a promotion does not change meaning between languages.

Plan the end of the campaign

Remove expired conditions, restore normal messaging and review fulfilment exceptions. Compare results with suitable periods and record changes in stock or traffic. Avoid presenting every seasonal sales increase as proof of a design improvement.

Plan what happens when the campaign ends

Define the stock, dispatch capacity and final ordering dates before preparing the landing page. Write down who updates sold-out offers and removes expired promises. Reuse the campaign address only with current content; old delivery deadlines can mislead visitors who arrive from saved links.
