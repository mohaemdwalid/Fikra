Doing business in Qatar

Where should you start when planning an online business in Qatar?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Start with the business you will operate, then confirm its registration route. Buying Shopify or a domain does not register that business.

In this guide
  1. Prepare a question the authority can answer
  2. Information to have ready for an official enquiry
  3. Describe the business accurately
  4. Verify the applicable route
  5. Coordinate the website project
  6. Take a specific description to the authority

Prepare a question the authority can answer

Write a short description of the products, where they are prepared or stored, who owns the business and how orders are delivered. A home-prepared product, imported goods and a service can raise different questions. Use MOCI’s current services and ask which activity and approvals apply to your circumstances. Keep the answer and required documents with the launch plan. You can prepare content meanwhile, but do not present platform setup as a substitute for the business process.

Describe the business accurately

Write down what you sell, who fulfils it, where the business operates and whether products or services have additional approvals. These details help you ask the authority a specific question instead of relying on a generic online checklist.

Verify the applicable route

Review current MOCI services and confirm the requirements for your circumstances with the relevant authority or a qualified adviser. Do not apply a rule quoted for a different activity, ownership structure or country without checking its relevance.

Coordinate the website project

Keep registration, banking, provider verification and storefront work on a shared readiness list. Let the website brief reflect the approved activity and verified business details. Do not publish a license number or compliance badge that has not been issued to your business.

Take a specific description to the authority

Describe what you sell, how you fulfil it and whether the project operates from home, a shop or another arrangement. Ask for the process that applies to that activity and retain the official response. A generic registration list found online may omit a condition relevant to your business.
