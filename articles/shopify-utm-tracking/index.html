Shopping experience and growth

How should you use UTM links for Shopify campaigns?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Use consistent campaign links and understand the report’s attribution rules. Tracking helps answer the question, but it cannot explain every influence on a purchase.

In this guide
  1. Keep a shared naming sheet
  2. Agree a naming convention
  3. Check the destination
  4. Compare reports with context
  5. Use names the whole team understands

Keep a shared naming sheet

Use stable source, medium and campaign names across creatives. Open the complete link and check that redirects or language changes do not lose the intended context. Never put personal information in campaign tags. Compare reports using the same attribution basis and period: a customer can discover you in one channel and return through another. Review paid or fulfilled outcomes alongside clicks so a busy campaign is not mistaken for a profitable one.

Agree a naming convention

Use stable source, medium and campaign values that your team understands. For example, distinguish a paid social campaign from an email announcement instead of using the same generic label for both. Keep a shared link sheet and avoid changing capitalisation or spelling between creatives accidentally.

Check the destination

Open the exact campaign link and verify that it reaches the intended page. Review whether campaign parameters survive any redirect or language switch where needed. Do not put names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal information in URL tags that can appear in logs and analytics.

Compare reports with context

Attribution models and reporting windows can assign credit differently. A customer may discover the brand in one channel and return through another. Record your chosen reporting basis and use it consistently. Review completed commercial outcomes as well as traffic so a high-click campaign is not mistaken for a profitable one.

Use names the whole team understands

Agree a small naming convention before publishing campaign links, then record the final URLs in one place. Check that navigation preserves the intended destination. Compare campaign reports with their known limits; a tagged visit is useful evidence but does not establish that one ad caused every later purchase.
