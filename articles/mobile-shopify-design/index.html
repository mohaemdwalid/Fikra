Shopping experience and growth

What makes a Shopify store easier to use on mobile?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Watch someone find and buy a product on a phone. Fitting within the screen is necessary, but it is not the whole experience.

In this guide
  1. Give the shopper a task, not a tour
  2. A small test someone else can perform
  3. Start with the buying task
  4. Keep overlays under control
  5. Test content extremes
  6. Watch someone shop without coaching

Give the shopper a task, not a tour

Ask them to find an item, inspect its size information and select a variant without coaching. Notice wrong taps and places they hesitate. Review sticky buttons, chat and consent overlays together; individually neat widgets can cover the same small screen. Use a long title, an unavailable option and Arabic labels if supported. The useful result is a completed, understood task, not merely a screenshot without horizontal scrolling.

A small test someone else can perform

Choose a size or colourA visible selection and a correct item in the cart.
Read delivery informationReadable text that does not disappear behind a sticky widget.

Start with the buying task

Ask a reviewer to find an item, inspect its details, choose a size and open the cart. Observe where they pause or tap the wrong element. Keep navigation labels clear and make option states visible. Do not infer success only from the absence of horizontal scrolling.

Keep overlays under control

Announcement bars, chat tools, cookie controls and sticky purchase buttons compete for a small screen. Review them together rather than approving each widget in isolation. Check that dialogs can be closed, text remains readable and important content is not covered by fixed elements.

Test content extremes

Use a long title, an unavailable variant and the longest translated label. Review menus and product galleries in both directions when the store supports Arabic. Shopify theme customisation provides the implementation tools; your acceptance check should focus on the customer’s actual ability to finish the task.

Watch someone shop without coaching

Give a person a specific product to find and ask them to reach the point before payment. Do not explain where buttons are. Note every pause, mistaken tap and question. These observations can expose a hidden size selector or delivery uncertainty that a polished screenshot will not show.
