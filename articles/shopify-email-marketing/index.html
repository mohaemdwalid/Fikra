Operations and next steps

What should you plan before Shopify email marketing?

By FIKRA · Updated 16 October 2026 · 2 min read

Send a relevant message to an appropriate audience with a clear purpose. An order email and a promotion are not the same kind of communication.

In this guide
  1. Check audience, promise and destination
  2. Separate message purposes
  3. Prepare a useful campaign
  4. Review outcomes responsibly
  5. Give each message one purpose

Check audience, promise and destination

Review the permission and unsubscribe arrangement for the actual campaign. Use a real offer and link to a page that explains the same conditions. Check dates, stock, language and the visible sender before scheduling. Test on a phone and review overlapping sending tools. Read results beyond opens: did recipients reach a useful page and produce relevant outcomes? Keep customer lists controlled instead of casually copying them between services whenever a new tool is installed.

Separate message purposes

An order update and a promotional campaign serve different purposes. Keep customer expectations clear and review the actual consent and unsubscribe arrangements. Do not assume every person who enquires wants ongoing advertising.

Prepare a useful campaign

Use an accurate offer, relevant products and a destination matching the message. Confirm dates and stock before scheduling. Test the email on mobile and check links, language and the visible sender.

Review outcomes responsibly

Compare delivery, relevant engagement and actual commercial outcomes. Check duplicate sending tools and avoid repeatedly messaging an unresponsive audience without reviewing the cause. Keep access and customer data controlled rather than exporting lists casually between services.

Give each message one purpose

Decide whether the email helps customers use a purchase, discover a relevant product or respond to an offer. Keep the next step clear and use only contact lists you are entitled to market to. Review unsubscribes and replies alongside sales so frequency does not become the entire strategy.
