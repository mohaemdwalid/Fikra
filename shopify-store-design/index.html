Shopify store design built around your brand.

FIKRA designs and develops Shopify stores for brands launching their first website or moving to a more considered shopping experience. We bring your brand identity, products and store structure together, then agree the pages and features your project needs.

What a Shopify design project can include

A project can cover the homepage, collection pages, product templates, navigation and essential store settings. The exact scope depends on your catalogue, content and existing setup. Custom features, paid themes and apps are discussed before they are included in your quote.

Start with the way customers shop

A useful store helps visitors understand what you sell, find the right collection and review a product before buying. We consider product information, photography, options, delivery information and calls to action together. On mobile, readable copy and clear navigation matter as much as the visual design.

From consultation to handover

The first conversation covers your products, audience, existing website and priorities. We agree the scope, review the design and develop the agreed Shopify pages. Before handover, we review the shopping journey and identify any remaining content or configuration work. Timing is agreed for each project.

What to prepare

Bring your logo, product photography, product descriptions, collection structure and delivery information. If you already use Shopify, share your store URL first. Access can be arranged when needed. Missing content and responsibility for supplying translations should be agreed before development starts.

Questions about this service

Do I need to buy a new theme?

That depends on your existing theme and the agreed design. We review the options first and identify any separate theme or app fees in the quote.

How much does a Shopify website cost?

FIKRA provides a project quote after reviewing the pages, catalogue and features required. Your Shopify subscription, paid themes and third-party apps may have separate costs.

Can you build an Arabic and English store?

A bilingual storefront can be included in the scope. Language content, right-to-left layouts and translation responsibilities are agreed during planning.
