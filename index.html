BEFORE WE TALK

Your questions, answered.

Can you build a new store or redesign an existing one?

We can discuss either. Your consultation helps us define the right scope for your store.

How much will my Shopify website cost?

The package price is being finalised. Your written quote will confirm the deliverables, payment terms and any separate platform costs.

How long does a project take?

We will agree on a delivery schedule after reviewing your requirements and the content available.

Can my store support Arabic and English?

Yes, we can include a bilingual storefront in the scope. We will confirm who supplies and approves the translations.

What should I prepare before we start?

Your logo, brand guidelines, product photos and descriptions, pricing, delivery information and access to your Shopify store if you already have one.

Who owns the store after delivery?

The store should be set up in your own Shopify account. Ownership, access and handover details will be written into the project agreement.

Is ongoing support included?

The support period and revision allowance are still to be confirmed. These will be specified in your quote.
